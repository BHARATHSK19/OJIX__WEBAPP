CORE ARCHITECTURE

High-Throughput Inventory & Lock Core

Engineered with distributed in-memory locking to guarantee zero seat collisions across high-concurrency booking streams and counter POS desks.

  • Distributed Redis In-Memory Locks: Millisecond seat reservation locks that release automatically upon checkout timeout, preventing phantom holds.
  • Dynamic Tariff & Yield Engine: Real-time algorithmic pricing adjusting fares based on departure lead times, competitor scraping, and festive rush patterns.
  • Multi-Channel PNR State Machine: Immutable booking lifecycle handling partial cancellations, seat swaps, and instant UPI refund disbursements.
CONNECTIVITY FABRIC

OTA, Telematics & GST Integration Fabric

Direct governmental e-invoicing pipelines and OTA sync paired with IoT telemetry from GPS and FASTag toll barriers.

  • Direct OTA & GDS OpenAPI Connectors: Native inventory broadcast across RedBus, AbhiBus, MakeMyTrip, and Cleartrip with universal sub-second manifest updates.
  • AIS-140 GPS & FASTag Telemetry: Real-time MQTT streaming ingesting coordinates, speed violations, unauthorized stops, and automated toll expense deductions.
  • Automated SAC 9964 GST Invoicing: Instant NIC e-invoicing generating IRN QR codes with automated Input Tax Credit (ITC) reconciliation for corporate clients.

What is Travel OS and who is it built for? +

Travel OS by RAKTIX and Eagle's Eye Destination is an all-in-one AI travel management system built for modern travel agencies, tour operators, DMCs, MICE planners, and corporate travel desks. It unifies enquiries, smart quotes, itineraries, visas, suppliers, fleet dispatch, and invoicing on a single platform.

How does the Smart Quote Engine calculate transport and tour fares? +

The Smart Quote Engine automatically calculates point-to-point distance and travel duration, applies contracted vehicle rates, and adds a customizable 10 km, 15 km, or percentage-based buffer to generate an accurate, ready-to-send quote in under 60 seconds.

Can Travel OS handle both international holiday packages and domestic fleet dispatch? +

Yes. Travel OS supports international multi-day itineraries for destinations like Dubai, Bali, Thailand, Singapore, and Europe, alongside domestic outstation cab quoting, live GPS vehicle tracking, and automated driver assignments.

How much time and revenue can a travel agency save with Travel OS? +

Businesses using Travel OS report up to 70% less manual administrative work, a 3X faster quote-to-booking turnaround time, and up to 40% higher profitability through automated margin protection, leakage prevention, and single-layer billing reconciliation.

Does Travel OS include a self-service portal for travellers and corporate clients? +

Yes. Every booking includes access to a Customer Travel Portal where travellers and corporate managers can view itineraries, upload passport and visa documents, download vouchers, and pay invoices online.
