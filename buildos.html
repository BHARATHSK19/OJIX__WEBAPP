CONSTRUCTION FAQS

BuildOS deployment.

Direct answers regarding offline mobile capability, subcontractor billing validation, ERP connections, and jobsite onboarding timelines.

Can site engineers submit Daily Progress Reports (DPR) offline? +

Yes. The BuildOS Field Companion mobile app works completely offline. Site supervisors log labor headcounts, equipment hours, poured concrete batches, and geotagged photographs. Data synchronizes automatically as soon as mobile connectivity is restored.

How does BuildOS prevent subcontractor over-billing on Running Account (RA) bills? +

BuildOS maintains a single source of truth through Digital Measurement Books (M-Books). Subcontractor bill line items are systematically validated against certified site measurements, pre-agreed contract BOQ rates, and maximum allowable wastage limits before payment vouchers can be generated.

Does BuildOS integrate with legacy ERPs like SAP and Tally? +

Yes. BuildOS features bi-directional REST APIs and webhooks that interface smoothly with SAP S/4HANA, TallyPrime, Oracle Primavera, and Microsoft Dynamics for real-time ledger sync and inventory journal entries.

How are site photos verified against fraudulent uploads? +

Every photo logged in BuildOS must be captured live through the in-app camera. The system embeds encrypted EXIF metadata with real-time GPS coordinates, compass orientation, timestamp, and device ID, completely preventing gallery uploads of old photos.

How quickly can a new construction project be onboarded? +

A standard project site can be fully operational on BuildOS within 5 to 7 working days. Our engineering deployment team uploads your master BOQ, configures user roles for PMs and site supervisors, sets up subcontractor master contracts, and conducts on-site training for engineers.
