What happens to biometric turnstile entry if the gym's broadband goes down? +

FitOS utilizes an offline local edge gateway. Mini-PC hardware installed on your LAN caches encrypted biometric hashes locally. Gates unlock within 180 milliseconds even during complete internet dropouts, synchronizing records once back online.

How does recurring UPI Autopay solve membership renewal chasing? +

Members authorize an NPCI UPI Autopay mandate upon joining. Monthly membership subscriptions debit automatically on due dates without awkward front-desk phone calls, recovering up to 34% in lost renewal revenues.

Does FitOS store raw facial images of members? +

No. Under India's DPDP Act 2023, FitOS never stores raw camera photos. Facial captures are instantly transformed into irreversible mathematical coordinate tokens (vector hashes) on the edge reader itself, ensuring member biometric confidentiality.

How are personal trainer commissions computed and verified? +

Personal trainers log sessions via mobile QR or client turnstile check-in verification. The system calculates split slab payouts, deductions, and client package balances automatically, eliminating spreadsheet confusion at month-end.

Can FitOS integrate with body composition scanners like InBody or Tanita? +

Yes. FitOS connects via Bluetooth and local network APIs to InBody, Tanita, and Accuniq scanners. Body fat percentages, visceral fat, and muscle mass readings sync directly into the member's personal mobile dashboard.
