How does automated 3-way matching work in ProcureOS? +

ProcureOS automatically cross-checks line items, quantities, agreed price rates, tax rates, and delivery milestones between the approved Purchase Order (PO), the warehouse Goods Receipt Note (GRN), and the vendor's GST e-invoice. If tolerances match within company policy, payment vouchers are generated automatically without manual data entry.

How does ProcureOS safeguard GST Input Tax Credit (ITC) for Indian enterprises? +

ProcureOS validates the vendor's IRN (Invoice Reference Number) and QR code via the National e-Invoice Portal and reconciles line items against your live GSTR-2B feed. Payments are released only when the vendor's GST filing reflects accurately, preventing ITC clawbacks and penalties under Section 16(2)(aa) of the CGST Act.

Does ProcureOS integrate with existing accounting software like TallyPrime, SAP, or Zoho Books? +

Yes. ProcureOS features pre-engineered bidirectional connectors and webhook APIs. Approved bills, payment vouchers, and inventory adjustments synchronize directly with TallyPrime, SAP S/4HANA, Zoho Books, or Oracle NetSuite without disrupting your core general ledger.

Can ProcureOS enforce multi-level purchase approval hierarchies (Delegation of Authority)? +

Yes. You can configure granular Delegation of Authority (DoA) matrices based on department, cost center, project code, or invoice value thresholds. Requisitions trigger instant approval alerts via email, portal, or SMS with full tamper-evident audit trails.

How does ProcureOS handle multi-location warehouse inventory and gate receipts? +

ProcureOS provides a mobile-ready Gate Inward module. Security and store managers record incoming vehicle numbers, physical gate passes, and perform batch/serial number inspections at the unloading bay, creating digital GRNs that immediately update inventory across all designated plant warehouses.
